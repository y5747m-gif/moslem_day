Frequently asked questions

Is VocalPure really free?

Yes. The player, vocal isolation, analyzer and karaoke mode are all free with no time limits. An optional “Supporter” tip inside the app removes the single download-page reminder — that's it.

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes. Every release is built reproducibly, signed with our official key, scanned for malware, and published with a SHA-256 checksum you can verify. The app only requests access to your music files.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

We're preparing our Play Store listing. Direct APK lets us ship AI-model updates weekly instead of waiting weeks for review. The APK is identical to what the store version will be.

Does vocal removal work offline?

Completely. The AI model downloads once (~120 MB) and then runs on-device — even in airplane mode. Your songs are never uploaded anywhere.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG and OPUS for playback and separation. You can export isolated vocals as WAV (lossless) or MP3.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android first. Join the beta channel above and enable “Notify me about iOS” in settings to get notified at launch.
