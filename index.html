Why VocalPure

A music player with superpowers

Everything you love about a premium music player, fused with adaptive AI stem separation — all inside one standalone app.

Auto Purify

Zero taps: every song is analyzed automatically the moment you import it, and its music is removed automatically on playback — only the pure voice plays. Turn it off in Settings or switch modes any time.

Two-Track Splitter

Every song is split into two tracks — 🎤 Vocals and 🎶 Music. Mute the instruments and keep only the lyrics, or mute the voice for karaoke. Mix both stems freely and export the result as WAV.

Standalone Music Player

A complete player in its own right: song library, search, playlists, favorites, shuffle & repeat, sleep timer, playback speed and a 5-band equalizer — its own UI, fully separate from this website.

Adaptive Isolation Engine

The engine analyzes each song's stereo balance with a real FFT and picks the strongest strategy per track — center extraction for standard mixes, a wide blend for spacious masters, frequency focus for mono files — with a live clarity score.

Karaoke Mode

Flip it around: mute the vocals and sing over the original instrumental — bass and kick are preserved so the groove never falls apart.

Offline & Private

Separation runs 100% on your device — MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG and OPUS. Your music never leaves your phone: no uploads, no tracking, no account needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this website a music player?

No — this site is just the official download page. The player is a standalone Android app with its own interface: library, search, playlists, now-playing screen and settings. Nothing on this page plays audio.

Is VocalPure really free?

Yes. The player, vocal isolation, karaoke mode and WAV export are all free with no time limits and no ads.

How is this different from Lark Player or other music players?

VocalPure plays your music just like any top player — library, playlists, favorites, shuffle & repeat, sleep timer and equalizer. The exclusive difference: it splits every song into two tracks (vocals + music), so you can remove the instruments and keep only the lyrics, or remove the voice for karaoke.

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes. Every release is built reproducibly, signed with our official key, scanned for malware, and published with a SHA-256 checksum you can verify. The app only requests access to the audio files you choose.

Does vocal removal work offline?

Completely. The isolation engine is built into the app itself — no model download, no cloud, no internet. Even in airplane mode. Your songs are never uploaded anywhere.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG and OPUS for playback and separation. You can export isolated vocals or custom mixes as WAV.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

We're preparing our Play Store listing. Direct APK lets us ship engine updates weekly instead of waiting weeks for review. The APK is identical to what the store version will be.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android first. Join the beta channel above to be first in line when iOS arrives.
