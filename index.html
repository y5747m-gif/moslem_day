Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is VocalPure really free?

Yes. The player, the vocal/music splitter, the equalizer and WAV export are all free with no time limits and no ads in the player.

Can I try the player here in my browser?

No — by design. This website is only the download interface; the complete music player lives inside the Android app. Install the APK and everything runs offline on your phone.

How does the vocal isolation work?

VocalPure analyzes each song the moment it plays. Stereo tracks get crossover-coherent center-channel removal: the voice (which sits in the middle of the mix) is subtracted from the vocal band only, while bass below 130 Hz and highs above 9 kHz pass untouched. Mono tracks get a smooth band-focused cancellation with no spectral hole. You can adjust the isolation strength live.

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes. Every release is signed with our official key and published with a SHA-256 checksum you can verify. The app requests no special permissions and never uploads your songs.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

We're preparing our Play Store listing. Direct APK lets us ship updates weekly instead of waiting for review. The APK is identical to what the store version will be.

Which audio formats are supported?

Import and play MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV, OGG, FLAC and OPUS. You can export isolated vocals, karaoke or your custom mix as lossless WAV.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. After installing the APK, everything — playback, splitting, equalizer, export — runs on your device, even in airplane mode.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android first. Join the newsletter from the contact link below to hear about iOS at launch.
