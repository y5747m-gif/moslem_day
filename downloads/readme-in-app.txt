VocalPure — in-app note
=======================

You are running the installed VocalPure app: a standalone Android music
player with its own interface, icon and navigation. Nothing in it is a
web page — everything works offline on your device:

  · Library: tap the "+ Add songs" button (bottom right) to import music
    from your phone. Filter All / Favorites, sort by date, title or artist,
    and open the ⋮ menu on any song for favorites, playlists or deletion.
  · AUTO PURIFY — each song is analyzed automatically the moment you add
    it, and its music (instruments) is removed automatically on playback:
    only the pure voice plays. Switch to Original / Karaoke / My mix on
    the player screen, or turn the automation off in Settings.
  · Mini player above the navigation bar; tap it for the full player with
    the clarity ring, equalizer and queue.
  · Export vocals-only / karaoke / custom mixes as WAV files, saved to
    Music/VocalPure on your device.
  · Settings → Appearance: Light (default), Dark or System theme.
  · Back button: closes the player / menus, returns to the Library, then
    keeps the app (and your music) running in the background.
