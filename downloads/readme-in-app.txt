VocalPure — in-app note
=======================

You are running the installed VocalPure app — a standalone music player,
fully separate from the download website.

Everything works offline on your device:
  · add songs from your phone (top-right ＋ button),
  · AI VOICE ENGINE — always on. The engine removes the music from every
    song BEFORE it plays: the track is rendered through the AI first, then
    the purified voice starts. There is no music mode, no stem mixer and no
    music level anywhere in the app. Recordings longer than 12 minutes still
    play through the live engine (still fully processed, never unfiltered),
  · a pinned control bar stays above the tabs on every screen — cover,
    title, render progress, play/pause and next/previous one tap away;
    the system notification does the same from outside the app,
  · the Now Playing screen shows the AI panel: render progress while the
    song is prepared, then the full-track voice/music analysis,
  · save the purified voice as a WAV file instantly (Music/VocalPure on
    your device) — the purified render is written straight out.
